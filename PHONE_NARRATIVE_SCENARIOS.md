#  DESIGN LESSONS
# ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY THESE SCENARIOS MATTER
──────────────────────────

1. PHONE DRIVES NARRATIVE
   Without phone system:
     Player never hears about bullying
     Events feel disconnected
   
   With phone system:
     Bullying is persistent, visible, consequential
     Player feels moral weight

2. MULTIPLE VALID ENDINGS
   Scenarios show 4+ ways to respond to single incident
   No "correct" choice
   All choices have reputation consequences
   Encourages replayability

3. MORAL COMPLEXITY
   Not simple "defend victim" → hero
   
   Outcomes include:
   • Defending too hard (creates new conflict)
   • Defending too late (damage already done)
   • Authority intervention (suppresses vs. solves)
   • Player becoming victim (perspective shift)

4. PHONE = EVIDENCE
   What player likes/posts/ignores becomes evidence
   At game's end, reputation system calculates ending
   Player's phone history determines outcomes
   Creates real narrative agency

5. NPC MEMORY
   NPCs remember what player posted
   Remember if player liked bullying posts
   Remember if player defended them
   Phone creates persistent relationship history

6. ESCALATION TRACKING
   Phone shows bullying escalation in real-time
   Player can watch consequence unfold
   Urgency emerges naturally
   No explicit "fail" state, just consequences
